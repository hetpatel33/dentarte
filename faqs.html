Frequently Asked Questions

Does Dentarte accept digital impressions?

Yes, we accept digital impressions from a variety of intraoral scanners, including Trios, iTero, and 3M systems. This allows us to create prosthesis with greater precision and efficiency.

How can I place an order with Dentarte?

You can place an order by either uploading your STL files directly through our Upload STL File portal or contacting us via email at dentarte201@gmail.com or phone at +91 95583-31632 to discuss your case.

Do you offer case planning support for complex cases?

Absolutely! Dentarte provides collaborative case planning assistance for complex prosthetic and implant cases to ensure optimal results for both function and aesthetics.

If you have more questions or need assistance, please contact us.
