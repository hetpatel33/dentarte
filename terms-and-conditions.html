Terms & Conditions

The cost for fabricating custom-made dental appliances is non-refundable.

Remake Policy

Eligible remakes will be done at no charge if received within 30 days of invoice date.

All original case materials, including models, impressions, and the dental prosthesis, must be returned for credit consideration.

New Cases Will Be Billed

New cases will be billed if remake is required due to any of the following:

  • There is a shade or product change different from the original request.
  • Lab questioned die, margin, impression or bite and was advised to complete case.
  • Lab requested a try-in, customer declined and asked for a completed case.
  • Abutment required reduction due to undercuts/clearance and/or the teeth were re-prepped.
  • The Restoration fits the master cast.
Warranty

Warranty is limited to the framework only.
